Privacy Policy

Your privacy and security are fundamental to our business operations.

Privacy Policy Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Palm Coast Locksmith ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards personal information gathered through our website, phone inquiries, and mobile service dispatches.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to fulfill lock, key, and security service requests. This may include:

  • Contact Details: Your name, phone number, and email address submitted via contact forms or phone calls.
  • Location Information: Service addresses or GPS locations required to dispatch mobile locksmith units to your property or vehicle.
  • Service Details: Information regarding your vehicle make/model, residential lock type, or commercial facility access specifications.

2. How Information Is Used

Information gathered is utilized strictly to:

  • Dispatch mobile locksmith technicians to your location.
  • Communicate estimated arrival times, service quotes, and order status updates.
  • Process service transactions and billing.
  • Maintain operational and customer service records.

3. Data Sharing and Protection

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. Data may only be shared with verified service technicians, dispatch personnel, or as required by applicable laws to comply with legal processes or protect property rights.

4. Security Precautions

We implement administrative and technical security measures to protect your personal details against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. However, no electronic transmission over the internet can be guaranteed as completely secure.

5. Contact Information

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to update your contact details, please reach out via our Contact Page.

Privacy, Terms & Legal Disclaimer: Palm Coast Locksmith provides mobile lock, key, and security assistance. All services are subject to identity verification to ensure property ownership. We collect contact and location details solely to fulfill service dispatch. By requesting service, you agree to our standard terms and conditions.
