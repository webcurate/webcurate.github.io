Commercial Locksmith Services

Advanced security systems, master keying, and high-security locks tailored for commercial properties.

Overview of Commercial Security & Access Solutions

Protecting corporate assets, retail inventory, sensitive paperwork, and staff members requires industrial-grade security infrastructure. Our commercial locksmith division partners with business owners, property managers, and facility supervisors to install, maintain, and upgrade heavy-duty hardware that complies with local fire and building safety codes.

Detailed Breakdown of Our Commercial Offerings:

  • Master Key System Design: Simplify key management while maintaining strict security hierarchies. Give department managers access to specific zones while executive staff retain universal entry.
  • Panic Bar & Exit Hardware Installation: Ensure safe, code-compliant emergency evacuation routes with heavy-duty push bars and commercial door closers.
  • High-Security Mortise & Cylinder Locks: Guard against unauthorized key duplication and physical lock picking with restricted keyway systems.
  • Commercial Rekeying & Staff Transitions: Swiftly rekey office locks or replace hardware following employee departures to maintain uninterrupted premise security.
  • File Cabinet & Office Desk Locks: Protect confidential records, cash drawers, and proprietary equipment with secure locking mechanisms.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a master key system and how does it work?
A master key system allows individual doors to be opened by their own dedicated change key, while a master key is configured to open multiple or even all doors within the designated facility structure.

Are your commercial panic bars fire-code certified?
Yes, all emergency exit hardware and push bars we supply and install meet strict local commercial building and fire safety regulations.
